# Remediation Status Checks
Burn and Churn Coffee IAM Access Review Lab

---

## Purpose

A dated record of each check on the remediation of access review findings. Each entry records what was checked, what was found, and what happens next. Entries are added, never rewritten.

---

## 2026-10-30: Program alignment review

**Checked by:** program alignment review (internal audit of the Burn and Churn security program), reported to Olivia Carter (Owner).

**What was checked:** the status of Q1 2026 findings F-001 to F-004 against their target dates (2026-03-31 to 2026-04-05) in the [remediation tracker](remediation-tracker.csv).

**What was found:**

| Finding | Target | Status found | Evidence of remediation |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-001 Terminated employee's accounts active | 2026-03-31 | Open | None |
| F-002 Excess Toast admin role | 2026-04-02 | Open | None |
| F-003 Inactive vendor account enabled | 2026-03-31 | Open | None |
| F-004 User without MFA | 2026-04-05 | Open | None |

All four findings were **about seven months overdue**, and the tracker had not been updated since the review. Three of the four were assigned to the outsourced IT administrator (Vertex IT Support). The program's POA&M states it is reviewed every two weeks, but there is no record of any review since the items were created.

**Actions:**

1. Findings marked **Overdue** and escalated to the Owner.
2. Each finding given a closure plan (see the tracker's `Closure_Plan` column), delivered through the planned changes CR-001 (Microsoft 365 migration) and CR-003 (IT brought in-house) on 2026-11-15.
3. The stalled remediation is recorded in the program as part of the justification for CR-003, and the missing POA&M reviews as a program finding.
4. The **Q4 2026 access review** confirms each closure with evidence.
